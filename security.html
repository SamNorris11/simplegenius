Security

Your team is going to use AI. They should.

But what are they giving it?

AI isn’t the problem.

AI makes work easier. It helps people get more done in less time.

If it helps someone finish at 4 p.m. instead of 6 p.m., they’re going to use it.

They want to go home sooner.

And the better AI knows the problem, the better it can help.

So people give it more.

A customer email.

A sales plan.

A company document.

A problem they’re trying to solve.

Something their boss told them.

Something only their team knows.

None of that is strange. It’s how you get better answers.

But when your team is doing that across public AI tools, your company has a new problem.

Where is all that information going?

Who controls it?

And how much of your business is being handed over just to get a better answer?

Your team needs AI. Give them your AI.

That’s part of why we built Simple Genius.

Simple Genius gives your company its own place to use AI.

Instead of your people feeding company information into public AI tools every time they need help, they can work with AI that already knows the business.

Your company information stays in a secure, private environment built for your company.

Your data is encrypted.

Your company controls it.

And it is not used to train someone else’s AI.

Your people still get what they wanted in the first place: AI that helps them work faster, think through problems, and get things done.

The difference is where they do it.

They do it inside Simple Genius.

Inside the four walls of the business.

Your people get AI. Your company keeps what it knows.
