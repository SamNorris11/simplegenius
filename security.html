Security

Your team is going to use AI.

They should.

AI makes work faster. It helps people get more done and spend less time doing work that shouldn’t take all day.

But what are they giving it?

The Problem

The problem isn’t AI.

The problem is company information leaving the company’s control.

  • Customer information.
  • Internal processes.
  • The knowledge your best people carry in their heads.

That context is valuable. Your company should know where it lives, who can use it, and what happens to it.

Reality

People want to get their work done.

If AI helps someone finish at 4 instead of 6, they’re going to use it.

That’s not the problem.The company just needs a better place to do it.

The Answer

Give them AI built around your company.

Simple Genius gives your team an internal environment for working with AI using your company’s own context.

  • Your business knowledge lives in one place.
  • Your team works with AI inside Simple Genius.
  • What the company learns stays with the company.
Your Data

Your company knowledge stays your company knowledge.

  • Your information is encrypted.
  • Your information is never used to train someone else’s AI.
  • You can take your information with you at any time.
Control

AI your business can actually control.

The goal isn’t to stop your team from using AI. It’s to give them a place to use it where company context stays connected to the company.

Your people get AI.Your business keeps its knowledge.

AI isn’t going away.

Your company information doesn’t have to go with it.
