Trent Hiott and Mark Combs have spent more than 40 combined years building, operating, growing and selling businesses, including multiple 8-figure exits.

They know what it’s like when the decisions ultimately come back to you.

The people. The problems. The opportunities. The pressure. Where the business is today and where you’re trying to take it.

Over the years, they learned that knowing what to do is rarely the whole problem.

The harder part is seeing the business clearly, determining what matters now, turning direction into action, and consistently following through.

So they took what they’d learned from decades of doing it themselves and built a repeatable way to help other business owners do the same.

That became the Simple Genius Process.

The Simple Genius Process.

Your business is unique. The Process gives you a repeatable way to figure out what matters, what happens next, and keep moving toward where you said you want to go.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    It starts with you.

    We talk about your business. Where you’ve been. Where you’re trying to go. What’s working. What’s not. And what’s putting pressure on you right now.

  2. 02

    Perspective

    Then we get to work.

    We bring together what we’ve learned about you and your business and help you see what can be difficult to see when you’re inside it every day.

  3. 03

    Game Plan

    We turn direction into action.

    We determine what needs to happen, in what order, and build an actionable plan around where you’re trying to go.

  4. 04

    Execute + Adjust

    Then we stay with you.

    You execute. We stay beside you, keep you accountable, work through problems with you, measure what’s happening, and adjust the plan as your business changes.

Your business. Your goals. Your path.

The Simple Genius Process exists to help you keep moving toward them.
