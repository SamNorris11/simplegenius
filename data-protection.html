How we handle your data

Four commitments, in plain terms.

Mutual NDA on every account.

Every customer engagement is automatically protected by a mutual NDA. Your inputs, strategies, and competitive intelligence are legally protected from the moment you begin.

Encrypted on AWS.

All data is stored on AWS with strong encryption at rest and in transit. We follow AWS security best practices and maintain strict access controls so only you and your authorized team members can reach your information.

Never used to train public AI.

Your company’s data, conversations, and strategic inputs are never used to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s. What you share with Simple Genius stays with Simple Genius.

Governance support for your team.

We support vendor security questionnaires, negotiated service agreements, and enterprise data governance requirements. If your organization has specific security review needs, our team will work through them with you.

The principle behind all of it

Security and ownership are not features. They are the foundation.
