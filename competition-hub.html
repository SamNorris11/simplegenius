Walkthrough in production

Your competitors move every week. New hires, new products, new positioning, new pricing. Most of it is public. Nobody on your team has the time to sit and watch it.

Competition Hub does. Every day. It pulls signals from LinkedIn, press releases, product pages, pricing pages, job boards, and public filings. Then it stacks them against what your competitors were doing a week ago.

You get a clean feed of what changed and why it matters. No dashboards to build. No reports to read. Just the moves, tied to the competitor who made them.

And when a signal matters, Strategic Insights takes it from there and tells you what to do about it.
