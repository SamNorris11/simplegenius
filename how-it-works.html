Step one

See it on your business.

Twelve questions about how your firm runs. Not a software inventory. We are looking for the reasoning behind pricing, client exceptions, and the decisions that still live in a handful of people.

What we need

Two minutes, your website, and three competitor names.

What you get

A score out of 48, the band it lands in, and the three places your record is thinnest.

2
Step two

Build the record.

Business Intake works through six areas of your operation. Start with what you already have. Your team supplies the operating detail, and you decide what belongs in the record.

What we need

About 45 minutes for the first pass. Your team can answer on their own time.

What you get

A draft Business Brain that you can read, correct, and approve before anything works from it.

Six areas, filling in as you and your team answer.

Your attention

An honest answer.

“I do not have time to become an AI project manager. I have a business to run.”

The record cannot appear the week a client asks for an answer. Building it is the work.

3
Step three

Make the record true.

Business Brain gives you a draft in plain language. Correct what is off, delete what does not belong, approve the rest. Nothing joins the company record until you say so.

What we need

A read-through and your judgment, one section at a time.

What you get

One approved version of the company that stays current, and gives every downstream tool the same ground truth.

Every finding arrives sourced, and waits on your approval.

4
Step four

Use the record before you decide.

Strategic Initiatives, Strategic Insights, Competition Hub, and Work Streams put the approved record to work before a decision becomes a guess.

What we need

The discipline of bringing the record into the decision.

What you get

Every decision made with the company’s written reasoning behind it.

Recommended moves tied to the part of the approved record that supports them.
