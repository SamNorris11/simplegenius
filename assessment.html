How the scan works

Open method. Nothing hidden.

Three plain steps. The scan does the work between them.

  1. 1

    Start with three fields.

    Company name, company website, work email. That is enough for the scan to begin reading your firm and its competitors in the background.

  2. 2

    Answer what only you know.

    A short set of questions about how your business actually runs. You see the progress readout as the scan works. You can stop at any time and the answers you gave are still used.

    Read the scan.

    You get back a written scan. Where AI is already moving in your market, where your competitors sit, and where the openings are. Every claim is sourced to what the scan read.
