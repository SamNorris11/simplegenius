Why we exist

We needed this before we could build anything useful.

Simple Genius exists because the same missing step kept showing up in our own businesses first, then in the firms we advised.

Where we started

We bought the tools first.

We were not studying a category. We were trying to get something useful from AI inside real businesses. So we did what most owners do: bought tools, asked for automations, paid for builds.

Every attempt hit the same wall. We had not gathered the company context first, so the tool had no business-specific ground to stand on.

The step underneath

Tools, automations, and custom builds are available on day one. All of them assume a written record of the business already exists.

What the work told us

The essential pieces were not written anywhere.

When we got into the detail, the parts that mattered were not in a document or a system. They were in people’s heads: the exception someone always makes, the reason a step exists, the client who has to be handled a particular way.

Financials tell you what happened. They do not preserve why the business makes its decisions.

Out of heads, into a record

What matters leaves private memory and enters one record your firm can read, correct, and keep current.

The second wall

Then we learned the record has to stay alive.

Gathering it once is not enough. A record goes stale unless someone keeps it current. A general model without that current record will describe a business that no longer exists.

Left alone

A stale record gives the tool an old company to reason about.

Held as true

Approved, kept current, brought to the work, and changed only when the business says it changed.

Nothing becomes truth until you approve it, and every change after that is a controlled update.

Who we are

Two operators who got tired of buying answers that could not know the company.

Trent Hiott

Trent runs the product and position. Years in employee benefits and insurance compliance taught him that a confident answer without the facts is still wrong. He taught himself the infrastructure to build the record AI needs to work from.

Mark Combs, CFA

Mark is a CFA who has built and sold businesses. He spent his career in financial services, where a number has to be defensible and a conclusion has to be tied to what is known.

We use it

We built the thing we needed.

Simple Genius uses its own record. We document how we work, correct it when it changes, and use that record before we ask a tool to help us decide.
