The Simple Genius Process

Know what to do next.

Running a business gives you no shortage of things to think about. The Simple Genius Process brings the business together, connects where you are to where you want to go, and turns that direction into what matters now.

Start a Conversation See how it works ↓

Your business is probably in more places than you realize.

Some of it is in your head. Some of it is in your team’s heads. Some of it is in spreadsheets, systems, notes, conversations and plans you haven’t looked at in months.

At the same time, you’re carrying goals, problems, opportunities and decisions that all compete for your attention.

01

01 / Conversation

First, we learn you and your business.

The Process starts with a conversation. Before we tell you what to do, we need to understand what you’re actually building.

Where are you trying to go? What does the business look like today? What is working? What isn’t? What are you worried about? Where do you feel stuck? What decisions are sitting on your shoulders?

We learn the business. We learn the goals. We learn the problems. And we learn the person responsible for all of it.

Before there is a plan, there is understanding.

02

02 / Perspective

Get the business out of your head.

When you’re inside the business every day, everything is close. Problems overlap. Priorities compete. New information keeps coming in. It becomes difficult to step far enough away to see the business as a whole.

The Process brings the important pieces together so you can look at the business instead of constantly looking from inside it.

Your business People Numbers Ideas Problems Goals Decisions

What felt scattered starts to become a picture.

03

03 / Direction

Where are you actually trying to go?

Once the business is in view, the next question becomes simpler: what are we trying to accomplish?

That might be a long-term goal. It might be a problem that needs to be solved now. It might be a decision you can’t seem to get comfortable with. It might simply be making the business work better than it does today.

The destination belongs to you. The Process helps make it usable.

04

04 / Game Plan

Turn direction into what matters.

Knowing where you want to go is not the same as knowing how to get there.

The Process turns the destination into priorities, decisions and actions. Instead of trying to work on everything at once, you can identify what actually deserves attention now.

  1. Where you want to go
  2. What has to change
  3. What matters most
  4. What happens next

Big goals become smaller decisions.
Smaller decisions become action.

05

05 / Daily

Know what matters today.

A plan is useless if it disappears into a document.

The Simple Genius Process keeps where you’re trying to go connected to what needs your attention now.

  1. Where we’re goingYour destination.
  2. What matters right nowThe priority.
  3. TodayThe next action.

The right thing to do today should connect to where you want the business to go tomorrow.

That is the point of the Process.

06

06 / Execute + Adjust

You run the business. We stay in the Process.

Simple Genius does not hand you a plan and disappear.

You execute. The business responds. New information comes in. Problems change. Opportunities appear. Decisions need to be made.

Simple Genius stays alongside the Process as that happens.

We review what is happening.
We help think through what changed.
We adjust what matters next.
And you keep moving.

Your business changes. Your plan should too.

Customers change. Employees change. Markets change. Opportunities appear. Problems you never expected become urgent. Goals evolve.

That is why the Simple Genius Process is not a one-time strategy exercise.

It is a living plan for your business.

Where you’re going stays connected to what matters now, and what matters now changes as the business changes.
