Know what to do next.

1

We have a conversation with you.

We start with a conversation. We learn how your business works, what’s going well, what’s not, where you’re trying to go, what problems you’re trying to solve and what decisions are weighing on you.

For maybe the first time, we start getting the business out of your head and into one place.

2

After the conversation, we bring it all together.

We bring back what we learned so you can see the business together: who you are, where you are today, what’s standing in the way, and where you’re trying to go.

3

Then we build your Process.

Based on where you are and where you want to go, we create the Process for getting there.

From what we learned in the conversation and what we now understand about your business, we build a Process designed to move you toward where you want to go.

4

You follow the Process. We stay with you.

  1. Execute
  2. Learn
  3. Refine
  4. Keep moving

The Process gives you what to work toward and what to do next. You execute inside the business. Simple Genius stays with you along the way.

You’ll have a weekly call with the Simple Genius team to review progress, work through decisions, and stay accountable to the Process.

See clearly. Decide what matters. Know what to do next.
