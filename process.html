01 Conversation

It starts with you.

Simple Genius begins by listening. Before anyone offers an opinion, we want to understand you, your business, and where you want to take it.

It is a real conversation. Not an assessment.

  • Who are you?
  • Where has the business been?
  • Where is it today?
  • Where do you want it to go?
  • What’s working?
  • What’s not?
  • What’s standing in the way?

What’s keeping you up at night?

We start there.

02 Perspective

Then we get to work.

We bring together everything we have learned about you and your business.

The result is perspective on your own business that is hard to create when you are inside it every day.

Not generic advice. An understanding of your business.

What we bring together

  • You
  • Your company
  • Your goals
  • Your challenges
  • Your priorities
  • Your market
  • Your competition
  • Your constraints
  • Your opportunities

03 Game Plan

What needs to happen next?

We turn direction into an ordered plan, built around where you said you want to go.

  1. What needs to happen?
  2. What happens first?
  3. What depends on what?
  4. What deserves attention now?
  5. What can wait?
  6. Where is the biggest opportunity?
  7. Where is the biggest immediate pressure?

The answers become an actionable game plan. What matters now, what comes next, and what can wait.

04 Execute + Adjust

A plan isn’t the finish line.

You execute. Simple Genius stays involved.

  • We maintain context.
  • We talk.
  • We observe progress.
  • We help hold you accountable.
  • We work through new problems.
  • We adjust as circumstances change.
