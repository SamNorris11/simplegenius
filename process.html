Know what to do next.

Your business is probably in more places than you realize.

Some of it is in your head. Some of it is in your team’s heads. Some of it is in spreadsheets, systems, notes, plans and conversations.

The Process starts by getting it together.

01

  1. Understand
  2. Organize + Plan
  3. Execute
  4. Keep moving →

01

01 / Understand

First, we learn you and your business.

We start with a conversation. We learn how your business works, what is going well, what is not, where you are trying to go, what problems you are trying to solve and what decisions are weighing on you.

For maybe the first time, we start getting the business out of your head and into one place.

02

02 / Organize + Plan

Then, we bring it all together.

Your business, in one place Who you are Where the business is What is working What isn’t The problems The goals Where you want to go

We bring back what we learned so you can see the business together: who you are, where you are today, what is standing in the way and where you are trying to go.

You confirm it.

Yes. This is our business. This is where we are. This is where we’re trying to go.

We build the plan.

  1. Where you are
  2. What matters
  3. What needs to happen
  4. Where you want to go

Based on your business, your problems and your goals, the Process turns that direction into priorities and actions designed to move you toward where you want to go.

03

03 / Execute

Know what matters now.

  1. Where you’re going
  2. What matters right now
  3. What do I need to do today?

The plan does not sit in a document. The Simple Genius Process keeps your goals connected to what deserves your attention now.

You execute. Simple Genius stays in the Process with you.

As you make progress, new information comes in. Problems change. Opportunities appear. Decisions change. Sometimes the goal itself changes.

The plan stays alive.

Plans change. Goals change. Businesses change.

The Simple Genius Process iterates and refines as you continue to execute.

  1. Plan
  2. Execute
  3. Learn
  4. Refine
  5. Keep moving

See clearly. Decide what matters. Know what to do next.

The Simple Genius Process helps owners see clearly, decide what matters, and keep the business moving toward where they want it to go.
